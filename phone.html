Add it in the ntfy app

  1. Install the ntfy app if you haven't yet: App Store or Google Play.
  2. Tap Copy topic above.
  3. Open ntfy and tap +.
  4. Paste the topic as the topic name, leave "Use another server" off, and tap Subscribe.
  5. On your computer, click Send test alert in Queue Cue's settings. It should arrive on this phone within a few seconds.
